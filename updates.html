Version 1.3.0 16 Nov 2025

⚔️ Dungeon Expansion & New Battle Menu

The world of Gotcha expands with new exploration systems, combat interfaces, and progression rewards. Here is everything included in this release:

🏰 New Content: Dungeons

  • Explore procedurally generated dungeons filled with rewards, surprises, and unique challenges.
  • Each completed dungeon grants loot and contributes to new dungeon badge progress.
  • Improved visuals and better progression tracking in deeper dungeon levels.

⚔️ New Menu: Battle

  • Introducing the new Battle Menu with dedicated combat panels.
  • Improved fight pacing with dynamic display of combat stats and effects.

🏅 New Dungeon Badges

  • Earn exclusive badges as you clear dungeons.
  • Higher dungeon tiers unlock rarer badge and contribute to long-term progression.

🐂 New Unique Gotcha: Asterion

  • Introducing Asterion, a legendary Minotaur-class Gotcha.
  • Automatically rewarded every 50 completed dungeons.
  • Features powerful defensive abilities.

🖌️ New Menu Assets

  • A new Assets for the menu, improved the style and quality.
  • Improved categorization and faster access to your collection.

🧩 UI Improvements

  • Enhanced layouts for menus, icons, panels, and pop-ups.
  • Better visual balance and readability across the interface.
  • Minor style refinements for cleaner navigation.

Version 1.2.1 10 Nov 2025

🧬 Affinity System & Skill Tree Expansion

🌿 Affinity System

  • Earn Affinity Hearts by keeping a Gotcha equipped for 24 consecutive hours.
  • Spend hearts in the new Affinity Tree to unlock permanent stat boosts and strengthen your bond with each Gotcha.

⚔️ Skill Tree Overhaul

  • A fully functional Skill Tree has been added.
  • Use earned affinity points to upgrade Strength, Defense, Agility, Dexterity, Health, and chance-based perks like bonus Pixel drops or Legendary Loot Discovery.
  • Customize every Gotcha’s growth path and create unique builds.

🧩 Gotcha Deploy Quantity Selector

  • Deploying Gotchas to patrol is now easier than ever.
  • Choose exactly how many Gotchas to send at once, streamlining team management—perfect for large collections.

⚙️ Additional Improvements

  • Optimized menu layout for a smoother gameplay experience.
  • Minor bug fixes and overall performance enhancements.
